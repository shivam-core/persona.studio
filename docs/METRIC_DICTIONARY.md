# Metric Dictionary — Persona Studio V1

**Project:** Persona Studio – Customer Segments & Campaign Planner for Marketers  
**Date:** 13 October 2026  
**Formula Engine Version:** `v1.0.0`

---

## 1. Core Principles & Distinctions

1. **Observed vs. Modeled vs. Measured:**
   - **Observed Metrics:** Calculated directly from completed, historical transactions and engagement logs up to the as-of date (e.g. historical AOV, orders in lookback, net sales).
   - **Modeled Metrics:** Generated by the economic scenario simulator and constrained budget allocator under user-specified or synthetic assumptions (e.g. expected visitors, expected purchasers, modeled contribution profit, campaign CAC).
   - **Measured Metrics:** Derived strictly from empirical randomized holdout test outcomes across assigned customer cohorts (e.g. intention-to-treat conversion rate, bootstrap confidence intervals, incremental contribution profit).

2. **Acquisition CAC vs. Cost per Repeat Purchaser:**
   - **Campaign CAC (Acquisition):** `(Media Spend + Attributed Fixed Cost) / Acquired Prospects`. Defined strictly when the targeted cohort consists of verified prospects (0 historical purchases before as-of date).
   - **Cost per Repeat Purchaser (Retention / Reactivation):** `Campaign Marketing Cost / Repeat Purchasers`. Applied to existing buyers with prior purchase history; never relabeled as acquisition CAC.

3. **Tax & Net Sales Accounting:**
   - Gross Sales = `Quantity * Unit List Price`.
   - Net Sales = `Gross Sales - Discount Amount - Refund Amount`. Exclusive of sales tax/GST.
   - Refund Amount $\le$ Gross Sales - Discount Amount. Monetary refunds do not reverse physical COGS in default accounting mode.

## 3. Simulator & Unit Economics (Deterministic Engine)

### 3.1 Paid CPC Delivery Model
- Unit contact cost: $u = \text{CPC} \times a$, where $a \ge 1$ is average clicks per unique visitor.
- Modeled unique qualified visitors: $q = \min(N, B / u)$.
- Actual media spend: $S = q \times u$.
- Unspent variable budget: $\Delta B = B - S$.

### 3.2 Direct Outreach Model
- Unit contact cost: $c$.
- Modeled qualified recipients: $q = \min(N, B / c)$.
- Actual outreach spend: $S = q \times c$.

### 3.3 Unit Margins & Contribution Waterfall
- Net unit price: $P_{\text{net}} = \text{list\_price} \times (1 - d)$ or $\text{list\_price} - \text{fixed\_discount}$.
- Expected purchasers: $Q = q \times p$.
- Capacity-capped purchasers: $Q_{\text{cap}} = \min(Q, \text{capacity\_units})$ if capacity specified, else $Q$.
- Net Revenue: $\text{Revenue} = Q_{\text{cap}} \times (1 - r) \times P_{\text{net}}$.
- Variable Costs:
  $$\text{VariableCosts} = Q_{\text{cap}} \times \left( \text{unit\_cogs} + \text{fulfillment} + f \times P_{\text{net}} + \text{refund\_handling} \right)$$
- Contribution Before Marketing: $\text{Contribution} = \text{Revenue} - \text{VariableCosts}$.
- Marketing Cost: $\text{MktCost} = S + F$ (where $F$ is fixed campaign cost applied once).
- Contribution Profit: $\text{Profit} = \text{Contribution} - \text{MktCost}$.
- Campaign CAC: $(S + F) / Q_{\text{cap}}$ (valid only for acquisition objective).
- Media ROAS: $\text{Revenue} / S$ ($S > 0$).
- Marketing ROI: $\text{Profit} / \text{MktCost}$.

### 3.4 Finite Horizon Subscription Model
- Horizon: $H$ months (default 12).
- Survival curve: $s_t = (1 - h)^{t-1}$ for $t=1..H$, where $h$ is monthly churn.
- Discount factor: $(1 + d)^{t-1}$, where $d$ is monthly discount rate.
- Cumulative revenue per acquired subscriber:
  $$\text{Rev}_{\text{sub}} = \sum_{t=1}^H s_t \times (1 - r) \times P_t$$
- Discounted contribution per subscriber:
  $$\text{Contrib}_{\text{sub}} = \sum_{t=1}^H s_t \times \frac{(1 - r)P_t - v - fP_t}{(1 + d)^{t-1}} - O$$
  where $O$ is upfront customer onboarding cost.

## 4. Randomized Holdout Experiment & Impact Metrics

### 4.1 Stratified ITT Estimation
For strata $s=1..K$ (defined by persona card):
- Strata treatment conversion rate: $\hat{p}_{T, s} = \frac{Y_{T, s}}{N_{T, s}}$
- Strata control conversion rate: $\hat{p}_{C, s} = \frac{Y_{C, s}}{N_{C, s}}$
- Strata lift: $\Delta_s = \hat{p}_{T, s} - \hat{p}_{C, s}$
- Overall standardized lift: $\Delta = \sum_{s=1}^K \left( \frac{N_s}{N} \right) \Delta_s$
- Standardized control baseline: $p_C = \sum_{s=1}^K \left( \frac{N_s}{N} \right) \hat{p}_{C, s}$
- Relative Uplift (%): $\Delta / p_C$ (if $p_C > 0$, else null)

### 4.2 Uncertainty & Hypothesis Testing
- **Confidence Intervals:** 95% Percentile Bootstrap CI computed over 2,000 seeded customer-level stratified resamples.
- **Hypothesis Test:** Exact stratified permutation test and 2-sample proportion z-test.
- **Inconclusive Rule:** If 95% CI contains 0, the result is reported as: *"Evidence is inconclusive at the 95% confidence level"*. It is never reported as *"no effect exists"*.
